# Approve and collect Feedback

## Intent

Criticism becomes append-only Feedback only through an exact human decision.

## Examples

- **Given** a Critic proposal with exact revision and hash, **when** the human approves it, **then** exactly one provenance-bound Feedback entry is appended.
- **Failure:** **Given** a rejected, stale or unapproved proposal, **when** Feedback is queried, **then** it contributes zero Feedback entries.

## Success goals

- Only deliberate human-approved criticism enters the Feedback Box.

## Failure goals

- No proposal is treated as Feedback before approval.

## Expected outcomes

- Append-only Feedback with exact proposal provenance.
