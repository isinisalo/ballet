# Form the final Product Snapshot

## Intent

Terminal product evidence is a factual projection of canonical project and runtime truth.

## Examples

- **Given** every Environment Action is done and finalization succeeds, **when** the Run reaches terminal completion, **then** a Product Snapshot projects commit, artifacts, statuses, approvals and evidence.
- **Failure:** **Given** the Environment is incomplete or blocked, **when** product data is requested, **then** no terminal Product Snapshot is invented.

## Success goals

- The final product is factual, immutable and inspectable.

## Failure goals

- No provider prose or partial Run becomes a Product Snapshot.

## Expected outcomes

- A recomputable terminal projection linked to canonical truth.
