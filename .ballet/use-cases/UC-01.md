# Define project direction

## Intent

The human establishes version-controlled WHAT and WHY before an Environment is authored or run.

## Examples

- **Given** the project needs a bounded outcome, **when** the human maintains Goals, ADRs and Constraints, **then** agents receive one explicit approved decision context.

## Success goals

- Direction is version-controlled and unambiguous.

## Failure goals

- No provider prompt becomes an alternative source of project intent.

## Expected outcomes

- A traceable Direction closure for authoring and execution.
