# Provide architecture decisions to agents

## Intent

Every Action receives the accepted decisions and constraints relevant to its bounded responsibility.

## Examples

- **Given** an Action traces to accepted architecture, **when** its immutable task context is composed, **then** the relevant Goal, ADR, Constraint and Use Case references are available to the roles.

## Success goals

- Architecture decisions reach every affected Action.

## Failure goals

- No Action relies on ambient or obsolete architecture context.

## Expected outcomes

- A hash-bound instruction and Skill closure with stable decision references.
