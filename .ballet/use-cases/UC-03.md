# Provide architecture decisions to agents

## Intent

Every Action can read the accepted decisions and constraints relevant to its bounded responsibility when its selected instruction or Skill explicitly requests them.

## Examples

- **Given** an Action traces to accepted architecture, **when** its selected instruction or Skill requires project direction, **then** the role reads the relevant canonical `.ballet/**` documents.

## Success goals

- Architecture decisions reach every affected Action.

## Failure goals

- No Action relies on ambient or obsolete architecture context.

## Expected outcomes

- A hash-bound instruction and Skill closure with explicit document-reading guidance.
