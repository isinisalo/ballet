# Describe and approve a Use Case

## Intent

Only an exact human-approved semantic revision may become executable project truth.

## Examples

- **Given** a draft Use Case with acceptance intent, **when** the human approves its exact semantic hash, **then** the approved revision may enter an Environment.
- **Failure:** **Given** an approved Use Case, **when** its semantic content changes, **then** approval is invalidated and execution is blocked.

## Success goals

- Only exact human-approved Use Cases are runnable.

## Failure goals

- No stale approval survives a semantic edit.

## Expected outcomes

- An immutable approval record bound to one canonical content hash.
