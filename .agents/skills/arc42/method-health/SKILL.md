# Method health

1. Aggregate Validation FAIL reasons, local retries, repair targets, repeated Open questions, stale/contradictory documents, drift, evidence gaps and manual-intervention reasons.
2. Keep unmeasured metrics explicit; do not fabricate a baseline.
3. For a proposed change record baseline, hypothesis, expected measurable result, risk and later evaluation point.
4. Automatically correct only a low-risk link, index, format, deterministic lint or deterministic test with no semantic change.
5. Require explicit human approval for Goal, ADR, Loop, permission, network, release/deploy, instruction or skill behavior changes.
6. Update the ledger only when new evidence or an approved change exists; avoid editorial churn.
